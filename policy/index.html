To protect platform performance and security for all estimators, the following abusive actions are strictly prohibited and will result in immediate suspension.   • Attempting to bypass trial metering, role access controls, organization seat limits, billing checks, authentication barriers, rate limits, or any other usage restrictions.
  • Reverse engineering, unauthorized decompilation, disassembly, or attempting to extract, copy, or reproduce proprietary calculation engines, formulas, system algorithms, source code, or trade secrets.

  • Data mining, bulk exporting, or systematic extraction of user data, project data, or platform content for purposes unrelated to your legitimate use of the Service.
