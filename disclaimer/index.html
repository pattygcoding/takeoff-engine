2Contractor Responsibility & Mandatory Field Verification

The user (contractor, subcontractor, or estimator) maintains sole and exclusive responsibility for verifying all project dimensions, quantities, specifications, and costs prior to submitting any bid, executing any contract, or ordering construction materials. Specifically, users acknowledge and agree that:

  • Plan & Specification Review: The user is solely responsible for cross-referencing all takeoff quantities against complete engineering plan sets, geotechnical soil reports, addenda, and project project specifications.
  • Subsurface & Geotechnical Conditions: Soil excavation volumes, trench depths, swell/shrinkage factors, and rock/water classifications must be verified by field borings and local conditions. Takeoff Engine's trench calculations are geometric estimations and do not account for subsurface rock, unlocatable utilities, or adverse trenching collapse.
  • Material & Labor Price Volatility: Material costs, supplier quotes, fuel surcharges, labor wage escalations, and regional market volatility must be independently confirmed with local suppliers and trade unions before bid submission.
