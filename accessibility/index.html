Accessibility

Takeoff Engine values accessibility for people with disabilities and welcomes feedback on its website and construction estimating tools.

Accessibility Approach

The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA, serve as our accessibility goal and guide our design and development efforts.

This statement describes our accessibility goal and contact process. It is not a certification or guarantee of conformance.

Read WCAG 2.2

Good-Faith Notice and Opportunity to Respond

If you experience an accessibility barrier, please contact us before pursuing a formal claim so we can investigate and work with you toward an effective solution. Where permitted by applicable law, you agree to provide written notice describing the specific barrier and allow us 60 days to acknowledge, investigate, and implement or discuss a reasonable solution before filing a lawsuit or other formal proceeding. This request does not apply to emergencies and does not limit any rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be limited.
