Privacy Policy & Data Handling

Effective Date: August 2026 • How Takeoff Engine protects, handles, and processes your data

1Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when creating an account, uploading takeoff spreadsheets, generating estimates, and configuring your contractor profile (such as name, email address, company branding, and project takeoff data). When making a purchase, payment details are securely collected and processed by our Merchant of Record, Paddle.

2How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to provide, maintain, and improve Takeoff Engine; calculate estimates; generate export documents; process transactions; send service notices; and provide customer support.

3Data Ownership & Confidentiality

You retain full ownership of all takeoff spreadsheets, project bids, rate libraries, and customer data uploaded to Takeoff Engine. We do not sell, rent, or trade your proprietary takeoff data or client proposals to third parties.

4Payment Processing & Merchant of Record

All subscription payments, invoicing, and tax calculations are securely managed by Paddle.com (Paddle Payments Ltd / Paddle.com Market Ltd), acting as Merchant of Record. Takeoff Engine does not store raw credit card numbers on its servers.

5Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard encryption, authentication safeguards, and secure database controls to protect your project data. You may request account and project deletion at any time through your account settings.
